Urban Jilla Arya Vysya Badminton Championship
Rulebook 2025

Tournament is conducted with 12 teams

  • Each team contains 11 players (11 Men)
  • Out of 12 teams each team will play TWO League ties with different teams. The league format is shown below:

Tie 1

A - G

B - H

C - I

D - J

E - K

F - L

Tie 2

A - L

B - K

C - J

D - I

E - H

F - G

Match Format

  • Each league match contains:
    • Men Singles (MS) — 2
    • Men Doubles (MD) — 6
    • Total matches — 8

Note:

  • 1. All the singles and doubles events are conducted for 21 points only (2 points difference is mandatory extension up to 30 points and no extension after 29th point. 30th is the golden point) throughout the tournament.
  • 2. In each team 3 players will get a chance to play 2 matches. Only one Player is allowed to play 2 doubles format.

Trump Matches

Each league match contains TWO TRUMP MATCHES. One trump is decided of your own and the second trump match is predefined mandatory for last doubles match.

The Tie Order

MS    MS

MD    MD

MD    MD

MD    MD (Trump mandatory for both teams)

Note:

  • 1. The player who plays the singles event is eligible to play the men's doubles event as their second match.
  • 2. No player is allowed to play 2 doubles matches in a single tie.
  • 3. Any player (i.e. A, B+, B) can be eligible for extra doubles only when their A+ player is dropped out or absent in their team and the same team owner can decide their own player to replace the A+.

Team Composition

Each team contains:

  • 1 A+ graded men player
  • 1 A graded men player
  • 3 B+ graded men players
  • 6 B graded men players

Auction Rules

  • Auction will be done in the Ascending Order (i.e. starts with B, B+, A and ends with A+, this year A+ player will also appear in the auction)
  • Each team will be funded with 150,000/- points. (Base price of each player is 1000 points irrespective of the grade and Rank)

Player Replacement Rules

  • If any player suddenly exits/is not available for the tournament, a new player will be allotted by committee member through a draw from the reserved player list.
  • If there are no reserved players available in the reserved list then the opponent owner will decide who to play from the absentees team from the same category.

Tie-Breaker

If any league match has a tie, there will be a single knockout match (men doubles for 21 points) conducted to decide which team will proceed to the next round (it applies to group exit round also).

General Rules

  • The Committee and Umpires' decision is final.
  • Tournament will start by sharp 9 AM
  • Pools draw will be decided on Oct 12th (tournament day)
  • We are not responsible if any of the team captain/owner is not available at the draws.
  • Breakfast and lunch will be provided on Oct 12th.
