Urban Jilla Arya Vysya Badminton Championship
Rulebook 2025

Tournament Structure

Tournament is conducted with 12 teams

  • Each team contains 11 players (11 Men)
  • Out of 12 teams, each team will play TWO ties with 2 different teams

Tie Format

Tie 1

A - G

B - H

C - I

D - J

E - K

F - L

Tie 2

A - L

B - K

C - J

D - I

E - H

F - G

Match Format

Each Tie contains:

  • Men Singles (MS) — 2
  • Men Doubles (MD) — 6
  • Total matches — 8
  • In each team, 3 players will get a chance to play 2 matches

Note: All singles and doubles events are conducted for 21 points only (2 points difference is mandatory extension up to 30 points and no extension after 29th point. 30th is the golden point) throughout the tournament.

The Tie Order

1. MS

5. MS

2. MD

6. MD

3. MD

7. MD

4. MD

8. MD (Trump mandatory for both teams)

Trump Matches

Each league match contains TWO TRUMP MATCHES:

  1. One trump is decided by your own team
  2. The second trump match is predefined mandatory for the last doubles match for both sides
    • The winner of this match is awarded with two points
    • The loser team will be deducted by one point
  3. You can't apply trump again for final doubles

Points System

  • Each team will get rewarded with 1 point for a regular match if they won
  • No points for a losing team
  • If a team wins their own trump match, they will be rewarded with 2 points
  • If a team loses their own trump match, the losing team loses 1 point
  • The winning team is rewarded with 1 point as usual

Tie-Breaker

In any scenario where the team score is tied, they have to play a MD match for 21 points. The winner will get awarded with one point and this point will be added to the total team score (this applies to decide the final 4 teams).

Team Composition

Each team contains:

  • 1 A+ graded men player
  • 1 A graded men player
  • 3 B+ graded men players
  • 6 B graded men players

Player Replacement Rules

  • If any player suddenly exits or is not available for the tournament, a new player will be allotted by the committee member through a draw from the reserved player list
  • If there are no reserved players available, then the opponent owner will decide whom to play from your team (should be from the same category)

General Rules

The Committee and Umpires' decision is final

  • Pools draw will be decided on Oct 12th (tournament day)
  • By 8:30 AM, breakfast will be arranged, followed by match draws system
  • We are not responsible if any of the team captain/owner is not available at the draws
  • Other than the players, either the captain or owner only one person is allowed to discuss clarifications throughout the tournament
  • Tournament will start by sharp 9 AM
  • Breakfast and lunch will be provided on Oct 12th
